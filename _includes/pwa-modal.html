Install KnowU on Your Phone

Native app store releases are coming soon. In the meantime, you can add KnowU directly to your home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — instant launch, offline ready, and completely free!

Apple Safari required: iOS only supports "Add to Home Screen" when opened directly inside the Safari browser.
  1. 1

    Open Safari

    On your iPhone or iPad, open Safari and navigate to web.knowu.app.

  2. 2

    Tap the Share Button

    In Safari's toolbar at the bottom of the screen, tap the Share button .

  3. 3

    Choose "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the share actions menu and tap Add to Home Screen .

  4. 4

    Tap "Add"

    Tap Add in the top-right corner. The KnowU icon will appear on your home screen and open full-screen like a native app!

Google Chrome recommended: Chrome and modern Chromium mobile browsers provide seamless one-tap PWA installation.
  1. 1

    Open Chrome

    On your Android phone or tablet, open Chrome and go to web.knowu.app.

  2. 2

    Tap the Menu (Three Dots)

    Tap the menu icon in the top-right corner of Chrome (or look for the "Install app" prompt in the address bar).

  3. 3

    Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"

    Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen) from the browser options menu.

  4. 4

    Confirm Installation

    Tap Install when prompted. KnowU will be added to your home screen and app drawer with fast standalone access!
