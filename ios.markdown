## How to Install as a Web App (PWA) on iPhone &amp; iPad

Adding KnowU to your home screen gives you instant access, full-screen view, and fast performance without waiting for the App Store.

### Step 1: Open Safari
Open the **Apple Safari** browser on your iPhone or iPad and navigate to [**web.knowu.app**](https://web.knowu.app){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.
*(Note: Adding to the Home Screen requires Safari on iOS).*

### Step 2: Tap the Share Button
Tap the **Share** button located in the toolbar at the bottom of your screen (the square icon with an upward arrow).

### Step 3: Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down the share menu options and tap **Add to Home Screen** ([+] icon).

### Step 4: Tap "Add"
Tap **Add** in the top-right corner. The KnowU icon will immediately appear on your home screen and run in standalone app mode!
